Our Story

Kinaiya was created to give Filipino-made games a platform where they can be discovered and appreciated. We aim to showcase the creativity and talent of local game developers by featuring games inspired by Filipino culture, stories, and imagination. Through Kinaiya, we want to help more players discover the unique experiences that Philippine-made games have to offer.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support local Filipino game developers by promoting their games and making them more accessible to players. We aim to give independent creators an opportunity to showcase their work while encouraging people to support and appreciate the growing Philippine gaming industry.

Our Vision

We envision Kinaiya becoming a platform that helps Filipino-made games gain recognition both locally and internationally. We hope to inspire more people to explore local games, appreciate Filipino creativity, and contribute to the growth of the country's gaming community.
